Virtual Release Circles

A guided space to exhale, release, and reconnect.

Release Circles are somatic group experiences for women who are carrying too much and need a safe, gentle place to slow down without explaining everything first.

What it helps with

Not talking yourself into calm. Practicing it in your body.

  • Unwind stress and stored tension.
  • Release what your body has been holding.
  • Reconnect with yourself in community.
  • Leave with one gentle next step.

Inside a circle

A calm rhythm for real life.

You do not have to perform, prove, or have the right words. The circle gives your nervous system a place to settle.

Arrive

Slow the Noise

Release

Guided Somatic Practice

Move through gentle body-based prompts designed to help stress complete instead of stay stuck.

Integrate

Return Gently

Leave with language, support, and one realistic way to keep caring for yourself after the circle ends.
