Personal Wellness

For years you've held it all together. This is where you let it go.

Three ways to work with Rachel — gentle gatherings, a monthly sanctuary, and one-on-one support. Start wherever feels right for you.

Virtual & In-Person Gatherings

Release Circles

A small pause can make a big difference.

Release Circles are gentle spaces where women can pause, breathe, and let go of some of what they've been carrying. Each circle includes simple movement, mindfulness, guided reflection, and calming practices that help you reconnect with yourself and your body.

There is nothing you need to fix and nothing you need to prepare. Just come as you are.

Learn How to Join a Release Circle

These circles are for women who:

  • Feel stressed or overwhelmed
  • Are carrying a lot of responsibility
  • Want more calm in their day
  • Need time to recharge and reset
  • Long for connection and support

Monthly Membership

The Release Room

For years you've held it all together. The Release Room is a place for you to finally let it go.

Your monthly sanctuary for nervous system support, emotional wellness, and sustainable self-care — created for women who are tired of just pushing through the day and are ready to feel more grounded, supported, and connected to themselves.
