A gentle moment to give yourself permission for what you need most right now.

Sometimes the most healing words are the ones
you were waiting for someone else to say.

✨ You are allowed to rest.

✨ You are allowed to need support.

✨ You are allowed to stop carrying everything alone.

This tool will help you create a personalized permission slip to remind you of what your heart, body, and mind may need today.

Takes less than 2 minutes.

Who are you creating this for?

Choose who needs this reminder today.

What does she need to hear?

Choose the permission you want to send her.

What name would you like on your permission slip?

What are you carrying today?

Choose the weight that feels closest. You do not have to explain it perfectly.

Underneath that weight, how are you feeling?

Choose the emotional state that resonates most.

What do you need permission for today?

You are allowed to need that.

How do you need the words to feel?

Choose the tone of support that feels best.
