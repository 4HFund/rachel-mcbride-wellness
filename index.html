Curious about how this works?

What does a 'Nervous System Reset' actually feel like? +
It feels like a release of tension you didn't even know you were holding. It might feel like a sigh of relief, a slowing down of your heart rate, or a sudden sense of mental clarity.
How is this different from regular life coaching? +
Most life coaching focuses on the 'mind' and logic. This work focuses on the 'body' and the biological stress response. We change the signal your body is sending to your brain.
Is this approach science-based? +
Absolutely. Our work is grounded in Polyvagal Theory and somatic psychology. It’s about biology, not just "good vibes."
