The Details

Common Inquiries.

What is nervous system coaching? +
It is a body-based approach to wellness. Instead of just talking about your stress (which can sometimes keep you stuck in it), we use somatic tools to physically change the state of your body from "stressed" to "safe."
Is this like therapy? +
It is complementary but different. Therapy often focuses on the "why" and the "past." Coaching is forward-focused and focuses on the "how"—specifically, how to regulate your physiology in real-time.
