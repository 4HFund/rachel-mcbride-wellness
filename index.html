Questions

Things women often wonder.

What's the difference between coaching and therapy? +
Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented — we work on where you want to go and what's in the way. Therapy, by contrast, is often focused on processing and healing the past. Both are valuable. My work is not a replacement for therapy, but many clients find that coaching, particularly nervous system coaching, complements their therapeutic work beautifully.
What is the Women's Circle and who is it for? +
The Women's Circle is a monthly virtual gathering open to any woman who is craving community, rest, and genuine connection. We come together to share, breathe, and practice simple nervous system tools. No experience is necessary — just a willingness to show up as you are.
How does the Online Membership work? +
Membership gives you ongoing access to a growing library of guided practices, workshops, and wellness resources — available anytime, at your own pace. It's designed for women who want sustainable, consistent support without a long-term commitment.
What does 1:1 coaching actually look like? +
Sessions are held virtually and typically run 60 minutes. We begin with where you are, explore what's in the way, and build practical tools and insights together. Most clients meet with me every 2–4 weeks, depending on their needs and goals. I limit my 1:1 client spots intentionally to ensure each woman receives the depth of attention she deserves.
I've tried other things and nothing has helped. Will this be different? +
That's one of the most honest questions a woman can ask. What I can tell you is that nervous system-informed coaching approaches the root of burnout and overwhelm differently — it works with the body, not just the mind. Many women who've felt "stuck" find this work opens doors that other approaches didn't. I always recommend starting with a connection call so we can honestly assess whether my approach is right for you.
